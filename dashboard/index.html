Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next — and what to do about it. 200 protocols, ranked by what a launch would be worth.

How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × chains. The peer multiple is the median market-cap/TVL of live-token protocols in the same category (global median when a category has fewer than 3 peers). Growth adjusts for 7-day TVL change, maturity for how long the protocol has run, chains for how many chains it spans. Every figure comes from the sealed snapshot alone — no hardcoded names.
